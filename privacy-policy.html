Privacy Policy

Gapless: Block Fit Puzzle
Last updated: November 1, 2026

This Privacy Policy applies to the mobile game Gapless: Block Fit Puzzle (the "App"), developed by ChandShi ("we", "us"). It describes what information is collected when you use the App and how it is used. If you have any questions, contact us at chandshigames@gmail.com.

Information we collect

The App does not require an account and does not ask for your name, email address, phone number or any other personal details.

Your game progress (levels, stars, hints and settings) is stored only on your device. We do not receive it, and it is removed when you uninstall the App.

Third-party services

The App uses the following third-party services. They may collect information automatically, as described in their own privacy policies.

  • Unity Analytics (Unity Technologies) - gameplay events such as levels started and completed, time spent, hints used and settings changed, along with device information (device model, operating system, language, approximate country) and a random identifier. We use this to improve the game and fix problems.
  • Unity LevelPlay, Unity Ads and Google AdMob - used to show ads. These services may collect your device's advertising ID, IP address, approximate location, device information and interactions with ads, in order to serve and measure ads and prevent fraud, and to personalise ads where you have allowed it.
  • Google Play Billing - processes in-app purchases. We only receive confirmation that a purchase is valid. We never see or store your payment information.

Privacy policies of these providers:
Unity: https://unity.com/legal/privacy-policy
Google: https://policies.google.com/privacy

Your choices

  • You can turn off analytics at any time in the App under Settings > Usage data.
  • If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, the App asks for your consent before personalised ads are shown. You can change your choice at any time under Settings > Privacy choices.
  • You can reset your advertising ID or opt out of personalised ads in your device settings (Settings > Google > Ads).

Children's privacy

The App is not directed at children under the age of 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided information through the App, please contact us so we can take the appropriate steps.

Data retention and deletion

Analytics data is kept by Unity in line with its own retention policy. You can request deletion of analytics data related to your device by emailing us. Turning off Usage data stops any further collection. Advertising partners retain data according to their own policies.

Security

Information sent by the services above is transmitted over encrypted connections (HTTPS).

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated date.

Contact us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, email chandshigames@gmail.com.
